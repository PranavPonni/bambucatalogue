SIMPLE, OPEN ESTIMATES

What goes into a print?

One 1 kg spool costs ¥3,400. That makes the filament rate ¥3.40 per gram.

50 g print×¥3.40 / g=¥170 filament
Material value and the final price

The displayed yen range is estimated filament value, rounded to the nearest yen. It is not a selling quote. Printing time, supports, colour-change waste, finishing, hardware, packaging, and delivery may add to the price.

Where do weights and dimensions come from?

The supplied screenshots show designs, but no weights, dimensions, or print files. The ranges are planning assumptions for small PLA prints at the proposed sizes; they have not been measured or verified in Bambu Studio. Exact weights and dimensions remain pending for all 73 designs.

We need the matching model and A1 mini print profile to attach a sliced filament estimate. Bambu Studio slicing documentation ↗

Sizes, colours, and what is included

Dimensions are proposed width × depth × height in centimetres, subject to model checks. The A1 mini build volume is 18 × 18 × 18 cm; fit, orientation, and delicate details still need checking. Colours depend on available filament. Reference photos may show multiple variants or styling props; each item states whether its estimate covers one piece or a set.

Flowers, plants, key rings, magnets, and lights are not included in filament estimates. Printed candle and lantern pieces are for LED lights only; incense designs need a separate heat-safe insert.
